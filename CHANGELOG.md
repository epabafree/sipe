# Changelog

## v0.4.0 — CAT 2023 Slots 2 and 3 (batches 3 and 4)
- Two more full papers: CAT 2023 Slot 2 and Slot 3, 66 questions each (24 VARC, 20 DILR, 22 QA), in original order. The deck now has all 198 questions of CAT 2023.
- 16 new set cards (8 RC passages, 8 DILR sets), with full set solutions and tables; a redrawn street map for the Slot 3 police-patrol set.
- 21 new formula and technique cards (58 in total), including factor counting, AM ≥ GM, cube identities, change of base, common terms of APs, boats and streams, pipes, false measures, regular polygons, timed routes and weighted aggregates. VARC technique cards now list every question of their type.
- Papers: one Practice / Timed mock card per paper (3 in total).
- The package is now a single cumulative `sipe.apkg`. Importing it over v0.2.0 keeps your progress.
- Source corrections: Slot 2 VARC Q22 answer is 4312; the Slot 3 DILR street map now includes the A–E street; Slot 3 QA Q3 reads “(a + 5)x + (b² − 15)y = 8b”.
