# Formula and technique cards

Front: the formula's name and the situation it's for. Recall it, then reveal.
Back: the formula, what each symbol means, when to use it, a trap to watch, a quick example, and the questions it appears in (“Seen in”).

- **Quantitative Aptitude:** sipe ▸ 05 Formulas & Concepts ▸ Quantitative Aptitude ▸ Algebra / Arithmetic / Geometry / Modern Maths / Number System
- **Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning:** sipe ▸ 05 Formulas & Concepts ▸ Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (timetables, distinct totals, weighted scores, two-way tables and more)
- **Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension:** sipe ▸ 05 Formulas & Concepts ▸ Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension: an approach for each question type (EXCEPT, purpose, detail, inference, weaken, missing sentence, odd sentence out, para-jumble, summary)

They're tagged by topic, so the Study Planner's “Topic + its formulas” recipe mixes them with real questions.
