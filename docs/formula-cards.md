# Formula and technique cards

The front names a formula and the situation it's used in. The back has the formula, what each symbol means, when to use it, a common mistake, a short example, and the questions where it appears.

They're under `05 Formulas & Concepts`, split by section and, for QA, by area. The VARC cards cover the approach to each question type (EXCEPT, purpose, detail, inference, weaken, missing sentence, odd sentence out, para-jumble, summary).

Formula cards share topic tags with the questions, so the Study Planner's "Topic + its formulas" recipe can mix them in.
