# FAQ

**Do I need add-ons?** No.

**Maths shows up as raw `\(x^2\)` text.** Update Anki. Current versions of Anki, AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile render it natively.

**The theme keeps resetting.** The choice is kept in the card's local storage, which some setups clear. Use Anki's night mode and leave the card on Auto.

**A question accepts two answers.** A few published questions have two defensible answers, for example 2023 Slot 1 DILR Q2. Those cards accept both and explain why.

**How do I redo a paper or a topic?** Open the paper under `06 Papers & Mocks` and choose Practice, or open a topic under `04 Practice by Topic`. Neither affects your reviews.

**I rated a paper or topic card by mistake.** It will come back when it's due. Until then you can open it from Browse with Preview, or use the Study Planner's "Revisit a paper" recipe.

**Are the difficulty labels official?** No, they're estimates.

**Found a mistake?** Open an issue with the ID from the bottom of the answer side, e.g. `2023-S1-QA-07`.
