# FAQ and troubleshooting

**Do I need any add-ons?** No. Everything is built into the cards.

**Maths shows as `\(x^2\)` text.** Update Anki; MathJax rendering is built into current versions of Anki desktop, AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile.

**The theme switch doesn't stick.** Choices are saved in the card's browser storage, which a few setups clear. Pick the theme again, or set Anki's own night mode and leave the card on Auto.

**Where are TITA answers checked?** On the card itself. Spaces, commas and ₹ are ignored, and numbers compare by value.

**A question has two correct options.** Occasionally a published question has two defensible answers (for example 2023 Slot 1 DILR Q2). Those cards accept both and explain why.

**Can I study only my mistakes?** Yes: use the [flags](flags.md) and the [Study Planner](study-planner.md) recipes.

**I want to re-solve a whole paper, or redo one topic.** Open the paper in **06 Papers & Mocks** and choose **Practice**, or open a topic in **04 Practice by Topic**. Both let you answer any question again as often as you like, and neither changes your reviews.

**I rated a practice card by accident.** No harm done. It will come back when it’s due, and in the meantime you can open it from **Browse** (select it and choose Preview), or with the Study Planner’s **Revisit a paper** recipe.

**Difficulty labels?** They're estimates, made while writing the solutions, not official data.

**Found an error?** Open an issue with the question ID shown at the bottom of the answer side (e.g. `2023-S1-QA-07`).
