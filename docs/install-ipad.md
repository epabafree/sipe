# Installing on iPad or iPhone

AnkiMobile is the official Anki app for iOS (a one-time purchase) and supports everything in the deck.

1. Install AnkiMobile Flashcards from the App Store.
2. Download `sipe.apkg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/epabafree/sipe/releases/latest), tap it and choose Open in AnkiMobile.

Without AnkiMobile, you can import the deck on a computer, sync it to a free [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net) account and study in Safari. AnkiWeb is meant for quick reviews, and some of the interactive parts (the toolbar, paper and topic cards) haven't been fully tested there yet.
