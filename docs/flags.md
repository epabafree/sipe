# Mistake flags

Anki's coloured flags are used for four meanings. They sync to all your devices, and the [Study Planner](study-planner.md) can filter by them.

| Flag | Meaning | Use it when | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 Red | Concept gap | You didn't know the idea or formula | Ctrl/⌘ + 1 |
| 🟠 Orange | Silly mistake | You knew it but slipped | Ctrl/⌘ + 2 |
| 🔵 Blue | Too slow | Right, but it took far too long | Ctrl/⌘ + 4 |
| 🟢 Green | New trick | The shortcut or approach was new to you | Ctrl/⌘ + 3 |

- **Phone:** use the flag button in the review screen's menu.
- **Clear a flag** by pressing the same shortcut again (or choosing “no flag”) once you've fixed the mistake.
- **Revise by flag:** in the Study Planner, use the recipes “Fix concept gaps”, “Silly-mistake drill”, “Speed work” or “Tricks to revise”.

Flags are entirely optional, and the cards don’t mention them, to keep the answer side calm. A card can't set a flag by itself (Anki doesn't allow it), so flagging is one tap or one shortcut.
